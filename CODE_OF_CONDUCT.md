# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of private prompts or credentials are
not acceptable.

Assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements. Critique code and
evidence, not people. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may
restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented, and mindful of user safety and privacy.
